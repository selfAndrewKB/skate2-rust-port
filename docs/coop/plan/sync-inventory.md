# The synchronization inventory

Generated by `tools/coop/sync_inventory.py` from `coop/world_stream.rs` and
`living_world/pedestrians/living_world_pedestrian_entity.rs`; the classes are the reviewed part
(a field the structs have and the script does not know fails the check). Front page:
[README](../README.md). The audit that measures it: `tools/coop/sync_audit.py`.

## What travels (the world stream, format 3)

- `EntityState`: `id`, `kind`, `owner`, `epoch`, `position`, `yaw`, `velocity`, `flags`, `motion`, `descriptor`
- `Descriptor`: `entity_key`, `model_key`, `recipe_id`, `colours`
- `PropState`: `locator`, `claim`, `position`, `rotation`, `velocity`, `resting`
- `Contact`: `id`, `force`, `direction`, `velocity`, `omega`

## What the crawl saw (2026-11-10, 115 missions)

Seat 1's live audit over every mission ([missions](missions.md), "Synchronization"): position
within 0.5 m 99.6 to 100 % of samples in every mission (the worst sample 11 to 24 m in eleven
missions, the bench's teleport frames, 10 at most a mission); yaw within 5° 88 to 97 %; speed
within 0.5 m/s 51 to 96 %, 75 to 82 % in most (open item 39); flags equal 100 % in every mission
but five (89 to 96 %) and the demo `m_6p_01_demo_first_job` (24.6 % on the crawl's build: its
puppets never stood up, fixed the next day, open item 50; 59 % after, the demo's placement, 49). Pedestrians:
up to 25 at once (12 owned, 13 puppets), stream peaks 2.5 to 6.7 kB/s a seat; props 0 known
everywhere (none streamed yet); contacts sent in 14 missions (up to 555 in the demo), applied by
the owner, 0 to 2 confirmed knockdowns; handoffs, promotions, demotions 0 throughout; stalled
round ticks 0 in all but four missions (12 at most, `m_1b_01_goto_school`), starved 0 in all but
three (10 at most). The ambient AI skaters are not in these counts: each console spawns its own
(open item 53). Nor are the challenge AI skaters (2026-11-11): a mission's pro, judges or
leaders are each console's own copies, selected from the same data at the same tick and
spawned at the same slot on every console, driven by each console's own AI on the same paths;
their state is not streamed and not audited ([kinds](topics/kinds.md), open item 45).

The census audit over the same logs (`tools/coop/sync_audit.py`, folded into the ledger's "census
audit" table): 103 missions with dumps, 7,543 dumps, 109,590 pedestrian pairs at the same round
tick; ownership never clashed (0 pairs with both seats owning); model agreed in every pair.
Compared at the same tick (so including the stream's delay, which the live audit does not),
position agreed within 0.5 m in a median 99.2 % of pairs (89 % at worst, the Slappy's photo set,
where the pedestrians walk), yaw within 5° in a median 91 % (69 % at worst), speed within
0.5 m/s in a median 71 % (51 % at worst; open item 39); flags and `down` agreed in 100 % in all
but six and two missions (the demo at 23 % and 33 %, open item 50). Entities one seat listed and
the other had not yet spawned or had culled: 1,135 on seat 1, 618 on seat 2. The divergences
still open at a mission's last dump were speed 1,515, position 460, yaw 308, flags 22, down 11.
