## Found and fixed: a puppet never stood up

A pedestrian another console owns (a puppet) never got up after a knockdown, whether its owner
knocked it down or this console predicted the hit. The crawl's demo mission showed it (puppets
down for the whole round); M6's logs had it too. Two causes, read in the code and seen in a
trace of the colliding state: a puppet's behaviour graph was ticked only while it collided, so
the exit that clears the knocked-down flag and frees the collision's intent packet never ran;
and the stale packets then clogged the queue so later hits were rejected.

Fixed in `coop-v0.9.2-puppet-standup` ([world](../topics/world.md), "A puppet stands up"):
the graph is ticked one tick past the collision, the owner's "up" ends a knockdown this copy
still plays, the intents are cleared at that close, and only knockdown contacts await
confirmation. Proof: the demo's census `down` agreement 33 % → 99.2 %; a diagnostic shove at the
waterfront confirmed by the owner at +22 ticks, the puppet up with its owner.

A first attempt (forcing the colliding behaviour's recovery) was reviewed, disproven on the
bench and reverted before the real cause was found; the second was reviewed with six findings,
all applied (one real: the parked intent packet).

## Tooling added

`tools/coop/crawl.py` runs each mission by kind (execute and win; execute and complete by name;
complete by name), takes `CRAWL_EXTRA` for probe variables, `CRAWL_WAIT_S` and `CRAWL_WIN_AT`
for a long intro, and ends a mission on its own `complete`. `tools/coop/crawl_ledger.py`
regenerates the ledger and the per-mission details from the kept logs and run reports, with the
census audit (`tools/coop/sync_audit.py`) folded in. The complete-by-name diagnostic logs its
result; `SKATE2_TRACE_COOP_PUPPET=1` traces a puppet's colliding state. All in
[tooling](../tooling.md).
