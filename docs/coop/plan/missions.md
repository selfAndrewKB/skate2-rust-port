# The mission ledger

**Scope:** every campaign mission's standing in co-op, one row a mission, derived from the crawl's
kept logs and run reports by `tools/coop/crawl_ledger.py` and regenerated after every crawl; the
Notes at the end are hand-kept and merged in. Front page: [README](../README.md). The crawl itself:
[tooling](tooling.md), "The mission crawl". The details, one section a mission:
[missions-detail](missions-detail.md).

**Pass criteria, fixed before the runs:** a mission passes when both seats' scripts reach
`challenge_cleanup` after the diagnostic win at round tick 1800, and the autosaves that follow carry
one hash (or none, for a tutorial that saves nothing). It stops when either seat's script is still
running at the bench's deadline (170 s), is not executed by name, crashes, or loses the server.
A stop is classified from the log by pattern; "unknown" is a finding. Every row names its evidence
(`../bench/evidence/crawl-<mission>-seat-N.log`, overwritten by the newest run; the run reports keep
each run's verdicts). Evidence lives beside the repository and is provisional in the ledger's sense;
what the ledger states is what those logs say.
